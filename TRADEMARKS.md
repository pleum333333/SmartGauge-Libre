# Name and logo / ชื่อและโลโก้

"SmartGauge Libre" is the name of this original project by khanatuch.

- Forks and modified versions **must use a different name** and must not present themselves as the official project.
  You may say "based on SmartGauge Libre" (that is the required credit — see NOTICE).
- Do not use the name in product listings, shop names, or ads.
